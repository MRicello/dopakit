Bad Brain Day Protocol

The procedure

1. Say it: "Today's a bad brain day." 2. Pick ONE small task. Do only that. 3. Can't? Shower. Eat. Outside 10 min. 4. Try the small task again. 5. Still can't? Maintenance day. Low-energy stuff. 6. No guilt. Tomorrow is another shot.

Overwhelm Procedure

1. Close all tabs. 2. Write down everything in your head. 3. Circle 3 things that matter this week. 4. Cross out everything else. 5. Pick the smallest step from circled items. 6. Do that one thing. Repeat.
