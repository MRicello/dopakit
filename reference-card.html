Daily Routine

Morning (5 min)

  • Open Dashboard
  • Add 3-5 tasks to Today
  • Identify hardest task — do it first

Focus Sprint (25 min)

  • One tab only
  • Phone face down
  • Timer running
  • 5 min break: dopamine menu

End of Day (3 min)

  • Check off completed tasks
  • Log 1-3 wins in "Did the Thing"
  • Quick note: where to pick up tomorrow

Weekly Routine

Sunday Reset (10 min)

  • Answer 4 planner questions
  • Review last week's wins
  • Pick 3 priorities for this week
  • Schedule body doubling sessions

Friday Review (5 min)

  • What shipped this week?
  • What got pushed?
  • One thing to change next week

Dopamine Menu

5 min (after 2 micro-tasks)
Stretch · Tea/coffee · Outside 60s · One song · 20 jumps · Cold water · Voice note
15 min (after focus sprint)
YouTube video · Saved article · Walk around block · Quick call
30 min (after milestone)

Bad Brain Day

  1. Say it: "Today's a bad brain day."
  2. Pick ONE small task. Do only that.
  3. Can't? Shower. Eat. Outside 10 min.
  4. Try the small task again.
  5. Still can't? Maintenance day. Low-energy stuff only.
  6. No guilt. Tomorrow is another shot.

Money Cheat Sheet

  • Log income same day it arrives
  • 30% of every payment → tax account
  • Expenses: log immediately
  • Monthly review: first Sunday of month
  • Quarterly taxes: Jan 15, Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15

Body Doubling

  • 60-90 min sessions work best
  • State what you'll work on at the start
  • Mute. Cameras optional. Work.
  • Mid-point check-in: 30 seconds each
  • End: say what you finished
  • Free option: Focusmate (3 sessions/week)

Content Batching

  1. Pick topic from idea parking lot
  2. Write messy first draft (no editing)
  3. Wait 2 hours. Then edit.
  4. Create social versions from the post
  5. Schedule or publish

1 blog post → 3 tweets → 1 LinkedIn post → 1 email. Repurpose, don't recreate.
